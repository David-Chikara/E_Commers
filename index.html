1. Product and profit target

Landed cost should include inbound transport to your stock. Enter the correct product GST rate. If product GST is already included in landed cost and is not claimable, do not count it again.

2. Customer price and shipping

Customer total = your listing price + customer-paid shipping. Customer-paid shipping is NOT automatically your income or expense. Enter seller-paid shipping only if the platform actually charges you. The calculator uses the higher of seller-paid freight and highest-zone freight as a safety cost.

3. Marketplace fees and taxes

TCS/TDS are shown as cash withholding/tax credits, not operating expenses. Enter actual rates from your seller statement. Only enter non-recoverable fees as expenses.

4. Returns and RTO risk

Returned stock is assumed resellable unless you enter an unsellable rate. Return/RTO percentages are forecasts, not guarantees.
